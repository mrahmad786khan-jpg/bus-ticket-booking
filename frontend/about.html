About SafarSathi

Making Bus Travel Simple, Safe & Reliable Across India

Our Story

Founded in 2024, SafarSathi is India's fast-growing online bus ticket booking platform. We bridge the gap between bus operators and millions of travelers, providing seamless booking, transparent pricing, and instant seat confirmation.

🚀 Our Mission

To digitize regional and intercity bus transport in India, offering every passenger a stress-free and reliable travel experience.

🛡️ Safety First

We partner only with verified AC & Non-AC bus operators who adhere to strict safety, punctual departure, and high hygiene standards.
