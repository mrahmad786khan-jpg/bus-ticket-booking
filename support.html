Frequently Asked Questions

How can I cancel or modify my booking?
You can view your active bookings under "My Bookings" page. For cancellations, contact our support team directly via phone or Instagram.
When will I receive my ticket confirmation?
Tickets are generated instantly upon successful payment with a unique PNR code.
How do promo codes work?
You can copy available discount codes from the "Offers" section and apply them during the ticket checkout step.
